    Character Submission Guidelines

    1. Must comply with the Discord Terms of Service.
    2. Real people only — no fictional characters.
    3. Private individuals whose main notability is a murder or similarly violent crime are not accepted, unless the crime occurred more than 40 years ago. Political leaders and public figures satirised for their exercise of power are a separate category and are unaffected by this rule.

    Submissions that do not meet these bars will be rejected without further discussion.
